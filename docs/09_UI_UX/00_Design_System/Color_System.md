## Warm Ink Theme

The Warm Ink theme shall prioritise premium contrast rather than maximum darkness.

The interface should resemble a professional engineering workstation instead of a gaming interface.

Primary Background

Warm Graphite

Secondary Background

Charcoal Slate

Cards

Slightly elevated graphite panels.

Gold

Represents confidence.

Emerald

Represents intelligence and market opportunity.

Red

Represents risk or negative movement.

The Orb shall remain the brightest persistent element on the dashboard.
