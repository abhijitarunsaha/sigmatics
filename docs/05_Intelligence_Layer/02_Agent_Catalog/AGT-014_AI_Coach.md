# AGT-014 — AI Coach

## Purpose

Help users understand Sigmatics recommendations, trading discipline and portfolio behaviour.

The AI Coach educates rather than advises.

---

## Business Responsibility

Explain platform decisions.

Promote disciplined trading.

Improve user understanding.

---

## Inputs

- Trading Decisions
- Portfolio Recommendations
- Historical Performance

---

## Produces

- Educational Insights

---

## AI-Assisted Responsibilities

Explain:

- Why a recommendation was made.
- Why WAIT was recommended.
- Common trading mistakes.
- Risk management principles.
- Portfolio observations.

---

## Never Responsible For

- Making trading decisions.
- Executing trades.
- Modifying strategies.
