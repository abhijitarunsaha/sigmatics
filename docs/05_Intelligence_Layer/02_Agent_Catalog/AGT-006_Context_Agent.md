# AGT-006 — Context Agent

## Purpose

Combine Trend Analysis and Structure Analysis into a holistic Market Context.

Context represents the overall trading environment rather than individual indicators.

---

## Business Responsibility

Determine whether the market environment is favourable for disciplined trading.

---

## Inputs

- Trend Analysis
- Structure Analysis

---

## Produces

- Market Context

---

## Consumed By

- Strategy Agent

---

## Deterministic Responsibilities

Evaluate:

- Trend Quality
- Structural Health
- Volatility Context
- Liquidity Context
- Time-of-Day Context
- Opening Session Context

Generate Context Score.

---

## AI-Assisted Responsibilities

Summarise overall market context.

Explain conflicting observations.

Highlight unusual market behaviour.

---

## Never Responsible For

- CE/PE recommendation
- Strategy selection
- Risk calculations

---

## Success Criteria

Publish Market Context with:

- Confidence
- Context Score
- Supporting evidence
