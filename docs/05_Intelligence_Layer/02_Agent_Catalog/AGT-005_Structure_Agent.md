# AGT-005 — Structure Agent

## Purpose

Analyse the structural behaviour of the selected market.

The Structure Agent identifies the quality of the market structure rather than the market direction.

---

## Business Responsibility

Determine whether current market structure supports disciplined trading.

Evaluate:

- Higher Highs
- Lower Lows
- Breakouts
- Pullbacks
- Consolidation
- Support & Resistance
- Range Quality

---

## Inputs

- Trend Analysis

---

## Produces

- Structure Analysis

---

## Consumed By

- Context Agent

---

## Deterministic Responsibilities

Calculate:

- Market Structure Score
- Breakout Quality
- Pullback Quality
- Consolidation Strength
- Structural Integrity

Publish Structure Analysis.

---

## AI-Assisted Responsibilities

Explain structural behaviour.

Highlight abnormal market structure.

Describe key structural events.

---

## Never Responsible For

- Trend analysis
- Strategy recommendation
- Risk calculations

---

## Success Criteria

- Structure classified.
- Structural confidence published.
- Evidence attached.
