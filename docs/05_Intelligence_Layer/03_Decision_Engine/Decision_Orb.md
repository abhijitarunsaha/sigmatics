# Purpose

The Decision Orb is the visual manifestation of the Sigmatics Decision Engine.

It does not represent loading, progress, or system status.

It represents cognition.

The Orb continuously reflects the current state of Sigmatics' intelligence as specialized analytical domains observe the market, build confidence and converge towards a quantified recommendation.

The Orb serves as the primary visual identity of Sigmatics' intelligence layer.

---

# Design Philosophy

The Decision Orb shall communicate intelligence rather than activity.

Its purpose is to reassure the user that Sigmatics is continuously observing the market, reasoning over available evidence and preparing recommendations.

The Orb shall never attempt to attract attention through excessive motion or visual effects.

Instead, motion shall communicate thoughtfulness, confidence and composure.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Orb shall always appear:

- Calm
- Intelligent
- Premium
- Trustworthy
- Purposeful

The Orb shall never appear:

- Flashy
- Distracting
- Gamified
- Mechanical
- Random

---

# Visual Anatomy

The Orb consists of five visual elements.

## Decision Core

Represents the cognitive centre of Sigmatics.

The Decision Core visualises Confidence rather than Opportunity.

---

## Intelligence Layers

Surrounding the Decision Core are multiple Intelligence Layers.

Each layer represents a specialised analytical domain.

Examples include:

- Market Structure
- Trend Intelligence
- Market Context
- Risk Intelligence
- Opportunity Analysis
- Option Intelligence

These layers collaborate rather than compete.

---

## Intelligence Nodes

Each Intelligence Layer exposes one active node.

Nodes illuminate as their respective domains contribute meaningful observations.

Nodes remain fixed in position.

They do not continuously orbit.

---

## Energy Connections

Subtle energy flows visualise communication between Intelligence Layers and the Decision Core.

Energy movement shall always represent information flow rather than decorative animation.

---

## Ambient Glow

The Orb emits a restrained ambient glow which increases with Confidence.

The glow shall remain soft and premium.

---

# Orb States

The Orb transitions through the following states.

| State | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Dormant | No active market observation. |
| Observing | Continuous market observation. |
| Building Confidence | Evidence is accumulating. |
| Actionable | Confidence threshold reached. |
| Monitoring | Active trade monitoring. |
| Cooling Down | Returning to observation. |

---

# Confidence Representation

The Decision Core visualises Confidence.

Increasing confidence results in:

- Brighter Decision Core
- Increased Intelligence Layer visibility
- Stronger Energy Connections
- Slightly stronger heartbeat

Opportunity is represented elsewhere within the interface and shall not influence Orb luminosity.

---

# Motion Philosophy

The Orb does not spin.

The Orb breathes.

Heartbeat animation represents continuous cognition.

The heartbeat shall remain subtle and uninterrupted during observation.

Only Confidence influences heartbeat intensity.

---

# Behaviour

The Orb is always alive.

During market hours:

- The Orb continuously breathes.
- Intelligence Nodes awaken independently.
- Energy propagates through Intelligence Layers.
- Confidence accumulates progressively.
- The Decision Core gradually illuminates.

The Orb never suddenly changes state.

Every transition shall be progressive.

---

# Actionable State

When the Decision Engine publishes an Actionable recommendation:

- The Decision Core reaches its maximum designed luminosity.
- Intelligence Layers become clearly visible.
- Energy Connections become more apparent.
- The heartbeat becomes confident rather than faster.

The Orb shall never flash or pulse aggressively.

---

# Review & Execute

When the user selects **Review & Execute**, the heartbeat briefly pauses before resuming in Monitoring mode.

This pause symbolises the transition from recommendation to human decision.

---

# Accessibility

Motion shall remain sufficiently subtle to avoid distraction.

Animations shall respect reduced-motion operating system preferences.

The Orb shall remain understandable without animation.

---

# Design Mantra

> The Orb does not think for the user.
>
> It shows how Sigmatics is thinking.
