# Product Principles

---

# Purpose

This document defines the fundamental principles that govern the design, behaviour and long-term evolution of Sigmatics.

These principles represent the product's constitutional values.

Every architectural decision, feature, AI capability, user experience enhancement and future release shall align with these principles.

When a conflict arises between a feature request and a Product Principle, the Product Principle takes precedence.

# Product Pillars

Sigmatics is built upon three complementary pillars of financial intelligence.

## Trading Intelligence

Assist users in identifying high-probability trading opportunities through quantitative analysis, AI-assisted reasoning and disciplined risk management.

## Portfolio Intelligence

Enable users to monitor, evaluate and optimise their investment portfolios through continuous analysis and actionable recommendations.

## Financial Intelligence

Provide meaningful reporting, performance analytics and long-term investment insights that transform trading activity into measurable financial knowledge.

---

# Principle 1
## Decision Intelligence over Automation

Sigmatics exists to improve financial decision-making—not to replace it.

The platform assists traders and investors by transforming complex market information into quantified, explainable and actionable intelligence.

The final investment decision always belongs to the user.

---

# Principle 2
## Human Authority is Absolute

No AI component shall autonomously execute trades, modify portfolios or alter investment strategies without explicit user approval.

Artificial Intelligence augments judgement.

It never replaces responsibility.

---

# Principle 3
## Every Signal Must Be Quantified

Market recommendations shall never rely upon intuition or opaque reasoning.

Every recommendation must be supported by measurable evidence including deterministic calculations, contextual analysis, statistical confidence and risk assessment.

The product tagline,

> Signals, Quantified.

is both a branding statement and an engineering commitment.

---

# Principle 4
## Explainability is Non-Negotiable

Every recommendation generated by Sigmatics shall be explainable.

Users should always understand:

- What was recommended.
- Why it was recommended.
- Which market conditions contributed.
- Which indicators supported the recommendation.
- What risks were identified.
- Why alternative actions were rejected.

Black-box recommendations are not acceptable.

---

# Principle 5
## Deterministic Before Intelligent

Where deterministic financial models can provide reliable answers, they shall always take precedence over AI reasoning.

Artificial Intelligence shall complement deterministic systems rather than replace them.

This ensures reproducibility, consistency and auditability.

---

# Principle 6
## Confidence Must Never Replace Evidence

Confidence scores are indicators—not guarantees.

Sigmatics communicates probabilities, not certainty.

Higher confidence reflects stronger supporting evidence, not future market certainty.

---

# Principle 7
## WAIT is a Valid Recommendation

The platform shall never recommend trades merely to remain active.

When insufficient evidence exists, the correct recommendation is:

WAIT.

Protecting capital is considered a successful outcome.

---

# Principle 8
## Risk Before Reward

Capital preservation always takes priority over opportunity capture.

Every recommendation shall undergo risk evaluation before becoming eligible for execution.

Risk is never an optional consideration.

---

# Principle 9
## AI Learns Continuously but Evolves Deliberately

Sigmatics continuously observes market behaviour and learns from historical decisions.

However, learning never modifies production behaviour automatically.

Every proposed improvement follows the controlled lifecycle:

Observe

↓

Learn

↓

Generate Hypothesis

↓

Backtest

↓

Paper Trade

↓

Human Review

↓

Deploy

Learning is automatic.

Evolution is governed.

---

# Principle 10
## Every Decision is an Asset

Every recommendation represents valuable knowledge.

Decision snapshots, market context, execution outcomes and post-trade analysis shall be preserved to improve future understanding, research and platform evolution.

Historical intelligence is treated as a strategic asset.

---

# Principle 11
## One Responsibility per Capability

Each capability within Sigmatics shall perform one clearly defined function.

Examples include:

- Market Intelligence
- Context Intelligence
- Strategy Intelligence
- Risk Intelligence
- Portfolio Intelligence
- Financial Intelligence

This promotes modularity, explainability and long-term maintainability.

---

# Principle 12
## Integration without Dependence

Sigmatics integrates with external broker platforms, market data providers and third-party services.

However, business logic shall never become tightly coupled to any external provider.

Every integration shall remain replaceable through clearly defined interfaces.

---

# Principle 13
## Enterprise Quality from Day One

Sigmatics is engineered as an enterprise software platform.

Documentation precedes implementation.

Architecture precedes development.

Testing accompanies development.

Observability accompanies deployment.

Quality is designed—not inspected afterwards.

---

# Principle 14
## Continuous Improvement through Measurement

Every major capability shall expose measurable outcomes.

Examples include:

- Recommendation accuracy
- Risk-adjusted performance
- Portfolio growth
- User engagement
- System reliability
- Latency
- Explainability coverage

Improvement is driven by evidence rather than intuition.

---

# Principle 15
## Trust Above All Else

The most valuable feature of Sigmatics is not artificial intelligence.

It is trust.

Users should trust that:

- recommendations are unbiased,
- calculations are reproducible,
- risks are clearly communicated,
- decisions are explainable,
- data remains secure,
- and the platform behaves predictably.

Every engineering decision should strengthen that trust.

---

# Closing Statement

These Product Principles define the identity of Sigmatics.

Technologies will evolve.

Artificial Intelligence will advance.

Financial markets will change.

New asset classes, brokers, analytical models and capabilities will be introduced.

However, these principles remain constant.

Every future version of Sigmatics shall continue to uphold its founding commitment:

> Transform market complexity into quantified, explainable and actionable financial intelligence.

**Signals, Quantified.**
